Privacy policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

Campus ROAM is an independent student project. It is not run or endorsed by Georgia Southern University. This page explains what the app collects, why, and how to remove it.

What we collect

  • Account: your school email address and a password. The password is stored in scrambled form by our database provider. We cannot read it.
  • Profile: your first name, campus, year, major and the interests you pick.
  • What you add: the classes you enter, the events you save, and the organizations and offices you follow.
  • Feedback: answers to the short pilot surveys and any feedback you send.
  • Usage, only if you agree: if you tick the analytics box, we record which feature you used and when, linked to a participant ID. You can turn this off in your Profile at any time.

What we do not collect

  • Grades, financial aid, holds or student ID numbers. The app never connects to university student records.
  • Your location. Weather is requested for the campus, not for you.
  • The contents of your class schedule as usage data.
  • Interests marked private. They stay on your device and are never sent to us.

How we use it

  • To show your day, your classes and events that fit your interests.
  • To measure whether the pilot is useful. We report totals, such as how many students used a feature. We do not report what a named student did.
  • To contact you about the pilot at your school email.

We do not sell your information, and we do not show advertising.

Who can see it

  • You: database rules let each student read and change only their own data.
  • The pilot team: the founder can see account and profile information to run the pilot and fix problems.
  • Offices and clubs that post events cannot see student data.
  • The university does not receive your information from us.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase stores accounts and data on servers in the United States.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app's files.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver fonts and code to your browser. Like any website, they receive your device's internet address when files load.
  • Open-Meteo provides campus weather.

If you tap a link to a map, a calendar or a university page, that site's own privacy policy applies.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When the pilot ends, accounts and their data will be deleted unless you choose to continue, and we will email you first.

Your choices

  • Delete everything: Profile, then Delete my account. This removes your account and all data linked to it.
  • Stop usage tracking: untick the analytics box in your Profile.
  • Ask a question or withdraw: email nm14372@georgiasouthern.edu.

Who can use it

Campus ROAM is for enrolled college students. It is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
