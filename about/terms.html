What Campus ROAM is

Campus ROAM is a free pilot app built by a student. It is an independent project. It is not an official Georgia Southern University service and is not endorsed by the university.

Taking part

  • Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time and delete your account from your Profile.
  • Use your own school email, and keep your password to yourself.
  • The pilot may change, pause or end. We will tell you by email if it ends.

Check important details at the source

The app gathers class, event, hours and deadline information from pages the university publishes. That information can be late, incomplete or wrong. Always confirm deadlines, exam times, room changes and opening hours with the official university source. Do not rely on Campus ROAM alone for anything important.

Ask ROAM

Ask ROAM answers only from published campus information and can miss things or be out of date. It is not advice. In an emergency call 911. For crisis support, call or text 988.

Fair use

  • Do not try to access other people's accounts or data.
  • Do not disrupt the service or use it to send spam.
  • If you post events for an office or club, post only accurate information you are allowed to share.

We may suspend an account that breaks these rules.

Your information

The privacy policy explains what we collect and how to delete it.

No guarantees

The app is provided as it is, free of charge, during a pilot. We work to keep it accurate and available but cannot promise that it always will be. To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for losses that result from relying on the app.

Names and logos
