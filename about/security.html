Security and accessibility

Last updated November 12, 2026

Campus ROAM stores only what a student types into it, protects every record with rules inside the database, and passed an automated accessibility audit with zero issues. It is an independent student project in a small pilot, and this page says plainly what is and is not in place yet.

What the pilot stores

  • School email, first name, campus, year, major and interests.
  • The classes a student enters, the events they save and the organizations they follow.
  • Survey answers and feedback.
  • Counts of which features are used, only for students who agree.

It never stores or reads grades, transcripts, financial aid, holds, student ID numbers, payment details or location. It has no connection to the university's student records or learning systems.

How it is built

  • The app is a set of static files hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no application server of its own.
  • Student data lives in one Supabase database in the United States.
  • Campus information comes from pages the university publishes publicly.
  • Every connection uses HTTPS.

Who can reach what

Permissions are enforced inside the database, so a modified app or a direct request cannot get around them.

  • Students can read and change only their own data, plus published campus posts.
  • Offices and clubs with posting access can create and edit only their own posts, for the organization they were approved for. They cannot see student data.
  • The pilot team manages posts and sees pilot totals.
  • Signed-out visitors cannot read any table.

These rules were tested: one student cannot read or change another's classes, cannot make themselves an administrator, and cannot edit campus posts.

Sign-in and keys

  • Students sign in with a school email and password, and confirm the address from a link sent to that inbox.
  • Passwords are stored only in scrambled form by the database provider.
  • The app carries only the database's public key, which grants nothing beyond the rules above. The administrative key is not in the app or in any public file.

Consent and deletion

  • Joining is voluntary. Usage counting runs only for students who opt in, and they can switch it off in Profile.
  • "Delete my account and data" in Profile removes the account and everything linked to it in one step.

The privacy policy has the details.

Accessibility

  • An automated audit against WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 at levels A and AA found zero issues on every student screen and on the posting page, in light and dark mode.
  • Checked by hand: keyboard use with a visible focus ring, no sideways scrolling on a small phone, touch targets of at least 24 by 24 pixels, reduced motion respected, and zoom never blocked.
  • Not yet done: testing with screen-reader users, and a formal accessibility conformance report.

Planned before any wider launch

  • A paid database plan with daily backups.
  • A dedicated service for sign-up emails.
  • Sign-in through the university's own login, where a school partners with us.
  • An independent security review and a written incident plan.
  • A written data retention schedule.
  • Written permission from each pilot school to use its published information.
